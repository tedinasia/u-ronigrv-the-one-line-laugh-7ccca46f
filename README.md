Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line Laugh"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f).

`main.py` (stdlib-only, deterministic, no network) writes a single funny
one-liner — at most 200 characters, one sentence — to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line: an umbrella that stays home every time it rains, as if it
were more afraid of getting wet than its owner. An everyday truth (everyone
has forgotten an umbrella) with a sharp reversal at the end: the one object
whose job is to face the rain is the one dodging it.
